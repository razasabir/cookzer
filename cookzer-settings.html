Household Co-Admins

Invite another adult in your household (e.g. your spouse) so you both have full access to the same Family Profiles and Meal Planner — either of you can add a dish for the kids, and you're both editing the same shared weekly plan, not separate copies.

Family Profiles

Add household members two ways: a no-login profile a kid can pick on a shared device to suggest a meal, or a friend who already has their own Cookzer account, linked directly so their name and avatar come along automatically. Visible to anyone in a group you share a meal plan with.
