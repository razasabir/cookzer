Homemade Chicken Karahi

Less chilli than the usual recipe, cooked down until the tomato and ginger melt into the oil — restaurant quality, no delivery fee.

SK
Sarah K.
· shared 2 hours ago
30 min
Total Time
4
Servings
Medium
Difficulty
🌶️🌶️
Spice Level
Ingredients
  • Chicken, bone-in pieces1 kg
  • Ripe tomatoes, chopped4 large
  • Ginger, julienned2 tbsp
  • Garlic, crushed4 cloves
  • Green chillies, slit3–4
  • Cooking oil1/2 cup
  • Red chilli powder1.5 tbsp
  • Coriander powder1 tbsp
  • Saltto taste
  • Fresh coriander & ginger, to finisha handful
Method
  1. Heat the oil in a wide, heavy pan (a karahi if you have one) over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and sear until the pieces are lightly browned on all sides, about 6–8 minutes.
  2. Add the garlic and half the ginger, stirring for a minute until fragrant. Add the chopped tomatoes and green chillies, then cover and cook until the tomatoes break down into a thick sauce, stirring occasionally, about 12–15 minutes.
  3. Stir in the red chilli powder, coriander powder, and salt. Cook uncovered until the oil visibly separates at the edges of the pan — that's the sign the masala is properly cooked through, not just simmered.
  4. Reduce the heat to low, cover, and let the chicken finish cooking in the sauce for 8–10 minutes, until tender and no longer pink at the bone.
  5. Finish with the remaining ginger and fresh coriander. Serve straight from the pan with warm naan or steamed rice.
