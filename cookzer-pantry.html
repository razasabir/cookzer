Pantry Challenge

Cook with what you've already got. Tell us what's in your kitchen and we'll match it against every recipe on Cookzer — no shopping trip required.

Separate each ingredient with a comma. The more you list, the better the matches.
Describe it however you'd say it out loud — "leftover", "half a", "some" all get filtered out automatically.
Built for using things up before they go to waste, not just for what's freshest — a wilting bag of spinach or last night's rice counts as much as anything straight from the fridge.
