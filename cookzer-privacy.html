Privacy Policy

Last updated September 17, 2026

Cookzer ("we", "us") is a social network for people who cook. This page explains what information we collect when you use Cookzer — on the website, and in the Android and iOS apps, which are thin wrappers around the same website — and how it's used.

Information you give us

  • Account info: your email address and password, or, if you sign in with Google or Facebook, the name, email address, and profile photo those services share with us.
  • Profile info: display name, avatar, cover photo, bio, and — if you choose to fill one in — your Kitchen CV (professional background, signature dishes, open-to-work status).
  • Content you post: recipes, photos, videos, captions, comments, ratings, meal plans, shopping lists, and pantry/leftover ingredients you type in.
  • Messages: direct messages and group conversations you send through Cookzer's messenger.
  • AI assistant conversations: messages you send to Cookzer+'s Cooking Ideas, Leftover Help, and Health & Nutritions chat features.

Information collected automatically

Basic technical information needed to run the service — such as sign-in sessions and timestamps on your activity (posts, comments, recipe saves) — so features like feeds, notifications, and "last cooked" streaks work correctly.

How we use it

  • To operate core features: your feed, recipes, meal planner, messenger, profile, and notifications.
  • To personalize suggestions, like pantry-aware recipe matches and Cookzer+ chat responses.
  • To keep the platform safe: enforcing blocks/reports and account security.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your content to train third-party AI models beyond generating the direct response to your own chat message.

Who we share it with

Cookzer runs on a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf, under their own security and privacy commitments:

  • Supabase — hosts our database, authentication, and file storage (photos, avatars).
  • Bunny.net (Bunny Stream) — hosts and streams video posts.
  • Anthropic — powers the Cookzer+ AI chat features; messages you send there are sent to Anthropic's API to generate a reply.
  • Vercel — hosts the website and its serverless functions.
  • Google / Facebook — if you choose to sign in with either, they share your basic profile info with us as described above.

Other users see what you'd expect from a social app: your public posts, profile, and anything you post in a group you're a member of. Direct messages are only visible to their participants.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete your posts, recipes, and profile info at any time from within the app.
  • You can block or report another user from their profile.
  • To delete your account and associated data, contact us at the email below.

Children's privacy

Cookzer is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at privacy@cookzer.com.
