Measure & Convert

Heard a measurement you can't measure? Convert it to whatever you've actually got in the kitchen.

Only needed to convert between weight and volume (like grams to cups) — exact weight-to-weight or volume-to-volume conversions work without it.

Common cooking conversions
3 tsp = 1 tbsp
16 tbsp = 1 cup
1 cup ≈ 237 ml
1 oz ≈ 28.3 g
1 lb ≈ 454 g
1 kg = 1000 g
